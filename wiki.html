Short bio

Wasim Ibrar (Md Wasim Ibrar) is an Indian digital creator, technology educator, and author. He produces short-form educational videos and builds web projects that make complex topics in artificial intelligence, technology, and science accessible to students and beginners. He is the author of the book The Future of AI: How to Learn, Work, and Earn with Artificial Intelligence.

Early life

Wasim was born on 07 November 2007 and hails from Darbhanga, Bihar, India. From an early age he showed interest in technology, web development, and explaining technical concepts to peers.

Career & Work

Wasim works as a content creator and developer focusing on:

  • Artificial Intelligence education for beginners
  • Short-form explanatory videos (YouTube Shorts)
  • Web development projects, including API-driven websites and portfolio sites
  • Practical guides and resources to help students prepare for technology-focused careers

He aims to simplify technical knowledge and inspire young learners to build practical digital skills.

Book

Title: The Future of AI: How to Learn, Work, and Earn with Artificial Intelligence

About the book: A beginner-friendly guide for students and young learners that explains fundamentals of AI, the impact of AI on future jobs, and practical steps to build applicable AI skills and career pathways.

Availability: The book is available on Amazon in both paperback and Kindle eBook editions.
